A View of the Season

The theatrical calendar

Day by day across 141 years, 1659–1800. Each cell is a night; deeper coloured cells are busier ones. The hush of Lent, the long summer stillness and the evenings when half-a-dozen houses lit up at once all appear at a glance.

Nights across the years

Rows are years; columns are days of the calendar. Hover a square for the date and the night's tally; click to read the bill. Only day-precise records are plotted, partial dates and season-only entries sit outside the grid.
