Whose Cast? draws on the co-casting graph: four performers linked to the same work in a given decade. Name the Decade uses each work’s decade-by-decade performance count to find its peak era. Fill the Bill tests knowledge of the mainpiece–afterpiece pairings that shaped each evening’s programme. Box Office or Bust? sets two nights from the same venue’s season against each other, one a strong earner, one a lean night. Whose House? tests knowledge of which playhouse a given performer called home in a given decade. Debut Decade traces careers to their first recorded appearance. Play’s Home asks the same question of the repertoire: at which house was a given mainpiece most often staged in a given decade? Long Runner or Flash? pits two contemporary plays against each other by their total performances across all 141 seasons. Opening Night traces plays to their first recorded London appearance.

Ten rounds per game. Questions are drawn at random from a pre-built bank. Data sparse before 1710; questions are drawn from the 1710s–1790s.
